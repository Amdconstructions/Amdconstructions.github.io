HOW WE WORK

A clearer path from idea to handover.

A straightforward process helps align requirements, scope, materials, timeline and finishing expectations before and during the project.

01 / DISCUSS

Understand the requirement

Share your property location, type of work, priorities, approximate area and budget expectations.

02 / PLAN

Agree the scope

Review the work scope, materials, sequence, estimated timeline and quotation before work begins.

03 / EXECUTE

Coordinate the work

Proceed with the agreed work and keep communication clear as the project moves through its stages.

04 / FINISH

Review and hand over

Review finishing details and discuss any final agreed items before handover.

BEFORE WORK STARTS

Clarity at the beginning helps the project.

Useful details to prepare

  • Property location and type
  • Service or work required
  • Approximate area or measurements, if available
  • Reference photographs, drawings or plans, if available
  • Preferred timeline and budget expectations
