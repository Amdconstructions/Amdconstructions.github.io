01 / ABOUT AMD

From the first plan to the final finish, make every detail count.

AMD Constructions and Paint Services brings construction and finishing services together to make projects easier to plan and coordinate. We work across building construction, civil work, painting, interiors and renovation, with an emphasis on clear communication and practical execution.

Every project has its own requirements. The scope, materials, timeline and pricing are discussed with the client before work begins.
